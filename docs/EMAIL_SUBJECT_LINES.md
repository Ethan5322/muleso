## Subject Line Formula (Use to write your own)

### High Open Rate Formulas:

1. **Question Format**
   - "Ready to [outcome] for {{company}}?"
   - "What if [desire] was [timeframe]?"
   - "{{name}}, quick question..."

2. **Curiosity + Number**
   - "3 reasons {{service}} companies fail"
   - "The {{number}}-step process we used for [client]"
   - "{{number}} common {{service}} mistakes"

3. **Specificity + Action**
   - "Following up: Your {{service}} quote"
   - "{{name}}, let's schedule your free call"
   - "Your results are ready"

4. **Benefit + Urgency (Light)**
   - "Don't make this {{service}} mistake..."
   - "Last chance: {{offer}}"
   - "One more thing before we move on..."

5. **Emoji + Personality**
   - Always use 1 emoji (not 2+)
   - Use relevant ones: 🚀 (launch), 💡 (idea), ✅ (done), 🔑 (secret)
   - Don't use: 🎯❌ (overdone), 💰 (sleazy)

## A/B Testing These Subject Lines

Test variations:
- Add emoji vs no emoji
- Shorten vs longer (test 30 chars vs 60 chars)
- Question format vs statement
- {{name}} personalization vs generic

**How to A/B test in Zapier/Make**:
1. Duplicate your email action
2. Use first version for 50% of contacts
3. Use second version for other 50%
4. Compare open rates after 48 hours
5. Use winner for follow-up emails

## DO's & DON'Ts

✅ **DO:**
- Use 1 emoji per subject (not more)
- Personalize with {{name}} if you have it
- Keep under 50 characters on mobile
- Create curiosity or promise benefit
- Test different variations
- Use all-lowercase if it fits your brand

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use ALL CAPS (looks spammy)
- Multiple emojis (dilutes the effect)
- "Hey", "Hi", "Hello" as opening (waste of space)
- Generic: "Update", "Message", "Important"
- Lies/misleading: Don't say "urgent" if it's not
- Beg: "Please read", "Don't ignore"

## Personalization Variables

You can insert these into subject lines (make sure your email service supports it):

- `{{name}}` - First name of contact
- `{{company}}` - Company name
- `{{service}}` - Service they inquired about
- `{{product}}` - Product they bought
- `{{time_ago}}` - "5 minutes ago" etc

Example: "Hi {{name}}, your {{service}} quote is ready"

**Make sure your Zapier/Make setup passes these variables correctly!**

## When Subject Lines Aren't Working

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Low open rate (<15%) | Add curiosity or personal touch. Test with emoji. |
| People open but don't click | Subject is off-topic from body. Fix email content. |
| Too many unsubscribes | Tone is too salesy. Make it more helpful/educational. |
| Gets marked as spam | Avoid: $, !!!, FREE, limited time (overused). Simplify. |

## Pro Tip: Pre-Header Text

Most email clients show 2 lines of subject + 30-50 chars of preview text.

Use this to reinforce your message:

**Subject**: "Got your message — here's what's next 🚀"
**Preview**: "We received your enquiry and will reply within 2 hours"

The preview should answer "Why should I open this?" If you don't set it, email clients show your first line of email body (usually ugly).

In Resend API, set preview text in the email body (first element that's visible but not the subject).
